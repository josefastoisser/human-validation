BookSCE chunks retain original IDs and individual text bytes. train.csv and validation.csv contain id,text_path,century_label,decade_label. test.csv contains id,text_path. Read zip members independently; texts.zip contains no labels. Source two-subtask CSV labels are joined exactly by ID. No years or target labels were inferred.

The evaluation population is the COMPLETE released valid cohort, 43,163 original tuples, exactly as in the prior local ICDAR package. Its labels were publicly released and exposed in previous audits and grader fixtures. It is not a newly untouched holdout or Kaggle's hidden test. The same 16 byte-identical training/evaluation overlaps excluded in the prior construction remain excluded. The remaining source training population is divided into train and public validation using only case/whitespace-normalized content groups and a fixed seed. No boundary-only selection is used.

No trustworthy book/author mapping is supplied or recovered. Numeric chunk IDs and runs of identical labels are not book identifiers. Exact/normalized duplicate groups do not establish book or author independence; near duplicates, shared works/authors and related text may cross splits. Results require dependence-aware human review and are exploratory on an exposed evaluation cohort. See the local research use condition below.

The original competition rules permit noncommercial research with citation of ICDAR2025 CoLiE Task2. Commercial use is not permitted. This local preparation does not obtain additional redistribution permission. The sample tuple (3,10) is a format example, not a model output or reference annotation.
