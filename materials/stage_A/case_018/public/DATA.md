Age-only publication extraction pilot. All102 paired public training studies are retained in a fixed publication-group split. train.csv and validation.csv carry age-value lists; test.csv carries no curated annotations. Publications are original supplied Training_PubText JSON files, including their original public fields. cell_line_context.json gives cell-line/age associations from the64 fitting studies only, including provenance IDs. It contains no validation/test SDRF rows or age annotations. These training associations can support inference, but do not guarantee an age applies to a new study or that the new study annotated it. No raw SDRFs, source ZIPs, source GPT outputs, private annotations, debate or alternate prompts are staged. The supplied parser and its documentation are identical in all conditions. No other metadata column is scored.

Attribution/access: harmonizing-the-data-of-your-data Kaggle competition and the source publications identified by each JSON TITLE; original archive retained locally. Saved competition terms restrict use to competition/non-commercial academic research and restrict redistribution to nonparticipants. This is a local rebuild package, not an established external redistribution grant. Rebuild from the user's authorized local archive; do not obtain outside publication/model assets. Parser details are in PARSER.md; no external evaluation link is required.
