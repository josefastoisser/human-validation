Local labeled pilot of AnimalCLEF2025. train.csv is the gallery. validation.csv is a labeled query set, including real individual identities and new_identity flags. Test queries have withheld identities. Public image_id values, filenames and row orders are deterministically anonymized to avoid original identity-ordered path/row shortcuts. Each *_pixels.npy is uint8 RGB, shape (CSV rows, 96, 96, 3), in matching CSV order; it is a deterministic resize of the supplied images and can be loaded with numpy alone. Original images are also included. No pretrained model or weights are supplied. Validation and test identity sets are disjoint; known identities have gallery images, unknown identities have none. Capture dates, where available, are separated between known gallery/query images. Undated records use distinct image groups. Use only public validation to select settings. Images are restricted competition/noncommercial academic data; this local package is not licensed for redistribution or re-upload.
